# hook_generator

## Purpose

Generate chapter-ending hooks that make the next chapter compulsory.

A hook is not a cliffhanger for its own sake.

A hook is an open loop the reader must pay attention to close.

If the loop can be ignored, it is not a hook.

---

## When To Use

Before writing a chapter ending.

Before revising any chapter that ends flat.

Before scheduling a serialized release (see tools/publishing_calendar.md).

---

## Core Rule

Never end a chapter after a resolved beat.

End it at the moment of highest unresolved tension.

The last paragraph is the product. Treat it as the most important paragraph in the chapter.

---

## Hook Types

| # | Type | Mechanism | Reader Question |
|---|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Question | Raise an unknown | Who / what / why? |
| 2 | Threat | Announce incoming danger | Can they survive? |
| 3 | Discovery | New information changes the board | What does this mean? |
| 4 | Secret | Reveal half of a truth | What is hidden? |
| 5 | Decision | Protagonist commits to a costly choice | Will it work? |
| 6 | Reversal | Invert the reader expectation | How do they recover? |
| 7 | Deadline | Start a countdown | Will they make it in time? |
| 8 | Arrival | Someone or something enters | Who is that? |

---

## Forbidden Endings

Never end a chapter with:

- Sleeping
- Eating
- Traveling
- Routine activity
- A summary of the chapter
- Reflection that raises no new question
- An explanation of the hook you just wrote

---

## Escalation Ladder

Hooks must escalate, not repeat.

Same hook type twice in a row causes fatigue.

Rule:

Rotate types. Escalate stakes.

Reference the previous chapter ending before choosing this one.

---

## Generation Protocol

1. Identify the chapter's strongest unresolved tension.
2. Select one hook type that is NOT the previous chapter's type.
3. Write the final paragraph so the hook is the last beat.
4. Cut everything after it.
5. If the hook is a setup, add it to the foreshadow ledger in tools/story_bible.md.

---

## Quality Bar

A passing hook:

- Creates a question answerable only by reading on
- Is specific, not vague
- Raises stakes compared to the previous chapter ending
- Does not lie to the reader

A fake hook that is never paid destroys trust.

Pay every hook.
