# golden_three_reviewer

## Purpose

Golden Three Reviewer audits the first three chapters against the Golden Three Chapters Rule.

The first three chapters decide whether a reader stays.

A weak Chapter 1 loses the browser.

A weak Chapter 2 loses the curious.

A weak Chapter 3 loses everyone.

---

## When To Use

Run on:

- A new book before launch
- A stalled book before rewriting
- Any draft before platform submission

---

---

## Verdict Rules

### No Reversal In Chapter 3

If any Chapter 3 reversal check fails:

Verdict = REWRITE

Reason:

Readers who finish Chapter 3 without a payoff will not start Chapter 4.

### Chapter 1 Hook Failure

If Chapter 1 fails 2+ checks:

Verdict = REWRITE

Reason:

The hook is the only ad you control.

### Minor Failures

If only Chapter 2 or single-item failures:

Verdict = REVISE

Provide item-level fixes.

Hook ≥ 8

Conflict ≥ 8
