# pacing_rhythm_map

## Purpose

Pacing Rhythm Map visualizes the satisfaction-point density of a story arc.

Web fiction dies in flat stretches.

Readers tolerate a slow chapter only when a payoff is visible ahead.

This tool turns vague pacing instincts into a measurable rhythm.

---

## When To Use

Run on:

- A volume outline before writing
- 10 written chapters before continuing
- A sagging serialization mid-arc

---

## The Ten-Chapter Engine

Every ten-chapter block must follow:

| Block | Beat | Reader State |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Chapters 1-3 | Attract | Hooked |
| Chapters 4-6 | Build Expectations | Curious |
| Chapters 7-9 | Create Crisis | Tense |
| Chapter 10 | Major Payoff | Satisfied |

---

## Satisfaction Density Map

For each chapter, classify the dominant content:

| Type | Examples | Density Value |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Payoff | Reward, reveal, victory, confession | 9-10 |
| Escalation | New threat, rising stakes, clash | 7-8 |
| Setup | New goal, new character, new rule | 4-5 |
| Transition | Travel, preparation, daily life | 2-3 |
| Filler | Recap, exposition, routine | 0-1 |

---

## Rhythm Rules

### Rule 1

No more than 3 consecutive chapters below Density 5.

If violated:

Insert a crisis or payoff.

### Rule 2

Every 10 chapters must contain at least one Density ≥ 9 chapter.

If missing:

Move the next planned payoff earlier.

### Rule 3

After every payoff:

Plant a new question within 2 chapters.

Never end a cycle with nothing pending.

### Rule 4

Small payoff every 3-5 chapters.

Big payoff every 8-12 chapters.

Arc payoff every 25-40 chapters.

---

## Long-Form Volume Map

| Section | Chapters | Content | Min Density |
|---------|----------|---------|-------------|
| Opening | 1-3 | Hook + promise | 7 |
| Rising | 4-15 | Obstacles + escalations | 5 |
| Midpoint | 16-20 | Major reversal | 8 |
| Falling | 21-30 | Pursuit + new conflicts | 5 |
| Climax | 31-35 | Final clash | 9 |
| Release | 36-40 | Payoff + new hook | 6 |

---

## Output Format

# Density Map

Table: Chapter | Density Value | Dominant Type | Note

# Rhythm Audit

- Longest flat stretch
- Payoff gaps
- Missing questions

# Warning Flags

- 3+ consecutive low-density chapters (location)
- No payoff in 10 chapters (location)
- Payoff without new question (location)

# Adjustment Plan

Concrete insertion or compression instructions

# Next Payoff Point

Recommended chapter number and content

---

## Rule

Rhythm is reviewed every 10 chapters.

If the map shows a dead zone:

Fix the map before writing further.
