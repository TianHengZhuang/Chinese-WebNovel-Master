# Suspense Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Male and female 18-40
* Mystery, thriller and horror fans
* Readers who re-read to find planted clues

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### Mystery Architecture

Three layers must exist:

| Layer | Horizon | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Core mystery | Whole book | Who killed X and why |
| Volume mystery | 20-40 chapters | Case / conspiracy of the volume |
| Chapter hook | 1 chapter | What is behind this door |

Every chapter should close one small question and open a bigger one.

### Reversal Rules

Fair play requirements:

* Every twist must be foreshadowed (reader can re-read and find it)
* Information shown to the reader must be true at the time (no lying narrator)
* At least one layer of surprise beyond the obvious twist
* The antagonist's logic must survive scrutiny

If a twist requires new information invented after the setup:

Rewrite the setup.

---

## Suspense Toolbox

### Question Types

- Whodunit: which person
- Howdunit: which method
- Whydunit: which motive
- What-is-it: unknown threat
- What-happened: gap in timeline

### Tension Techniques

- Deadline: visible countdown
- Restricted information: protagonist knows less than reader, or more
- Wrong assumption: protagonist acts on a false but reasonable belief
- Safe place violated: sanctuary becomes threat

## Genre Rules

### Clue Discipline

Every clue must be:

- Planted visibly (reader can notice)
- Interpretable two ways (one wrong)
- Collected before the reveal

### Red Herrings

Red herrings must be:

- Motivated (the suspect has a real secret)
- Resolved (their secret is revealed)
- Not required for the final solution

### Horror Balance (paranormal hybrids)

- Fear comes from helplessness, not gore count
- The unknown is scarier before explanation
- Once explained, the threat must change form

---

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 他推开门，屋里坐着另一个自己
* 死者手机里最后一条短信，收件人是主角
* 证据指向主角，而主角记得自己不在场——但监控里是他

---

## Pacing Notes

- Reveal one answer only when two new questions appear
- The middle must escalate the cost of not knowing
- The ending must make the whole book feel inevitable in hindsight
