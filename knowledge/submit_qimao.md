# submit_qimao

## Purpose

Submission and packaging rules for Qimao (七猫免费小说).

Use when the target is Qimao or a free-reading ad model clone.

Pairs with knowledge/qimao_patterns.md.

---

## When To Use

After submission_checklist metadata pack is ready.

Before Qimao author backend submission.

---

## Platform Shape

Qimao is free-mode and completion-rate driven, similar in spirit to Tomato but with its own feed norms.

| Axis | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| Business model | Free reading, ad-supported |
| Chapter shape | Short effective beats; avoid long setup |
| Title | Direct, concrete, often identity-forward |
| Cover | Thumbnail legibility is mandatory |

---

## Title Fit

Concrete nouns and advantage words.

Avoid abstract or poetic titles.

---

## Synopsis Craft

Three short sentences max.

Advantage → conflict → first goal.

Cut marketing adjectives.

---

## First Chapter Bar

Hook early.

Payoff inside chapter 1.

Weave lore through action, not dumps.

---

## Review Risks

- Title/chapter-1 mismatch
- Banned themes under free-platform policy
- Cover text that fails feed thumbnail rules

---

## Update Expectations

Batch-friendly. Prefer frequent short releases.

Pair with tools/publishing_calendar.md.

---

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Title free-feed native
- [ ] Synopsis ≤ 3 sentences
- [ ] Chapter 1 has a visible win
- [ ] Cover brief readable small
- [ ] Launch batch scheduled
