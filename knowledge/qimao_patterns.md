# Qimao Novel Success Patterns

Version: 1.0

Purpose:

Understand the free-reading business model and what keeps readers on the page.

Focus on:

- Completion rate (完读率)
- Click-through rate
- Session length
- Ad revenue compatibility

---

# Core Principle

Qimao runs on a free-to-read, ad-supported model.

Readers pay with attention, not money.

Every chapter must earn its keep:

- Instant hook
- Fast pacing
- Frequent emotional hits
- No slow buildup

---

# Reader Profile

Mainstream readers skew toward mass-market mobile users.

Characteristics:

- Short attention span
- Binge-reading behavior
- Sensitive to slow openings
- Strong preference for straightforward wish fulfillment

Less literary, more emotional.

---

# Preferred Genres

High-frequency categories:

- Urban power fantasy
- System novels
- Rebirth and revenge
- Village/farm-to-riches stories
- Short-form suspense
- Lighthearted romance (female side)

Genre choice matters less than execution speed.

---

# Opening Strategy

## First 500 Words

Introduce:

- Protagonist
- Conflict
- Curiosity
- Promise

All within 500 words. No world-building, no backstory.

## Recommended Opening Types

1. Humiliation + counterattack setup
2. System activation with immediate reward
3. Survival crisis with countdown
4. Rebirth with knowledge advantage

---

# Pacing Rules

Chapter length: 1500-2500 words, shorter is safer.

Every chapter must contain:

- One payoff or partial payoff
- One new hook at the end
- Visible progress

Never spend a full chapter on:

- Travel
- Preparation
- Conversation without stakes

---

# Emotional Rhythm

Payoff every 1-2 chapters.

Cliffhanger every chapter end.

Major arc resolution every 10-15 chapters.

Readers on free platforms churn fast. Feed them constantly.

---

# Title Patterns

Titles should telegraph the fantasy immediately.

Formula:

Identity + Situation + Promise

Examples:

- 重生2002：从收废品开始当首富
- 开局被离婚，我转身娶了女总裁
- 捡漏：从地摊开始无敌

---

# Commercial Optimization

Qimao distribution favors:

- High early completion rate
- Strong first three chapters
- Consistent daily updates
- Clear genre positioning

Avoid:

- Slow literary openings
- Ambiguous genre tags
- Long author's notes

---

# Success Checklist

Before publishing:

□ First 500 words contains a hook

□ Chapter 1 ends with a cliffhanger

□ Protagonist gains something visible in chapter 1-2

□ Every chapter has at least one emotional hit

□ Title telegraphs the core fantasy

□ Pacing is fast enough for mobile reading

If any are missing:

Revise.
