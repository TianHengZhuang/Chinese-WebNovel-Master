# submit_feilu

## Purpose

Submission and packaging rules for Feilu (飞卢小说网).

Use when the target is Feilu or a close extreme-pacing clone.

Pairs with knowledge/feilu_patterns.md.

---

## When To Use

After submission_checklist hard gates pass.

Before Feilu create-work / chapter upload.

---

## Platform Shape

Feilu optimizes for extreme pacing and payoff density.

| Axis | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| Reader patience | Very low; setup must collapse fast |
| Title style | Identity + cheat + concrete stakes in few characters |
| Chapter economics | Short bursts, frequent small wins |
| Fan-fiction culture | Known IP remixes are common but policy-sensitive |

---

## Title Fit

Lead with advantage and status.

Avoid slow literary openings in the title itself.

Numbers and system words help when they match the content.

---

## Synopsis Craft

Sentence 1: disadvantage.

Sentence 2: the extreme advantage.

Sentence 3: the first visible win the reader gets soon.

Do not promise a payoff that lands after chapter 30.

---

## First Chapter Bar

Conflict and advantage inside the first screen.

A concrete win or humiliation reversal before the chapter ends.

Zero world-tour openings.

---

## Review Risks

- IP / fan-fiction policy edges
- Clickbait title with no matching early payoff
- Category mismatch with power-system weight

---

## Update Expectations

High cadence. Use publishing_calendar safety line strictly.

---

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Title carries advantage + stake
- [ ] Synopsis promises near-term payoff
- [ ] Chapter 1 delivers a visible win
- [ ] Launch batch hooks scored
- [ ] Update dates fixed
