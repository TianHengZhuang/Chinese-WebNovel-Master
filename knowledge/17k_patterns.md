# 17K Novel Success Patterns

Version: 1.0

Purpose:

Understand the veteran all-genre platform with a low barrier to entry.

Focus on:

- Broad genre coverage
- New-author friendliness
- Stable serialization
- Mainstream male and female audiences

---

# Core Principle

17K is a platform for steady, reliable storytelling.

It does not demand genre-bending innovation.

It rewards:

- Clear genres
- Competent craft
- Consistent updates
- Stories that know what they are

---

# Reader Profile

Reader base is broad and mixed.

Characteristics:

- Genre-loyal
- Tolerant of familiar structures
- Appreciative of solid execution
- Sensitive to abandoned stories

---

# Preferred Genres

All-major coverage:

- Urban
- Xuanhuan
- Sci-fi
- Military
- Historical
- Modern romance
- Suspense

---

# Opening Strategy

Open with:

- A clear genre promise
- A protagonist worth following
- A conflict that defines the story's engine

Do not experiment in the first ten chapters.

Readers decide early whether the story is "theirs."

---

# Serialization Rules

Stable daily updates are the primary competitive edge.

Recommended:

- 2 chapters per day
- 4000-6000 words total
- Fixed update windows

---

# Story Engine

Every story needs a repeatable engine:

- A goal to pursue
- Obstacles that escalate
- Progress that is visible

If the engine stalls, the story dies.

---

# Commercial Optimization

Success factors:

- Clear genre tags
- First 10 chapters as a promise of the whole
- Regular updates during the first month
- Author activity in comments

---

# Title Patterns

Titles should be plain and clear.

Formula:

Subject + Situation + Promise

Examples:

- 都市之最强系统
- 特种兵王在都市
- 从军火商到世界首富
- 重生之商途

---

# Success Checklist

Before publishing:

□ Genre is unmistakable

□ Story engine is defined

□ First 10 chapters deliver the core promise

□ Update plan is realistic

□ Opening creates an early decision point

□ Long-term arc exists beyond the first arc

If any are missing:

Revise.
