# Ciweimao Novel Success Patterns

Version: 1.0

Purpose:

Understand the ACG (anime, comic, game) fiction community platform.

Focus on:

- Niche reader alignment
- Concept novelty
- Community interaction
- Fan-driven retention

---

# Core Principle

Ciweimao is an ACG/light-novel style platform.

Readers are not chasing pure power fantasy.

They chase:

- Interesting concepts
- Character chemistry
- Meta humor
- Genre mashups
- Community in-jokes

---

# Reader Profile

Readers are younger and deeply embedded in ACG culture.

Characteristics:

- Familiar with anime tropes
- Enjoy references and parody
- Highly responsive to chapter comments
- Value author-reader interaction

They read with a smile, not with clenched fists.

---

# Preferred Genres

Strengths:

- Original light novels (轻小说)
- Fan fiction and derivative works
- Daily-life fantasy
- Reincarnation into game/anime worlds
- Supernatural slice of life
- Comedy and parody

---

# Concept Design

The premise is the product.

Strong concepts include:

- Unexpected occupation (e.g., a villain, a side character, a house)
- Genre inversion
- Game-system twists
- Meta commentary on web fiction itself

Write the concept so a reader can pitch it to a friend in one sentence.

---

# Tone and Voice

Recommended tone:

- Light
- Self-aware
- Occasionally absurd
- Warm at the core

Dialogue carries most of the personality.

Avoid:

- Heavy melodrama without setup
- Overwrought prose
- Grimdark realism without comic relief

---

# Chapter Design

Chapter length: 2000-3000 words.

Each chapter should contain:

- One fun interaction or gag
- One plot movement
- One warm or exciting beat

Readers here reward character moments as much as plot progress.

---

# Community Optimization

Chapter-end notes can be playful.

Engage with:

- Reader theories
- Favorite characters
- Poll questions

A responsive author retains readers better than a polished one.

---

# Title Patterns

Titles should sound like anime episode names.

Formula:

Whimsy + Promise

Examples:

- 转生成蜘蛛又怎样
- 我的女友是丧尸
- 我在异世界当魔王幼崽
- 成为小说里的路人甲后我躺平了

---

# Success Checklist

Before publishing:

□ Concept is pitchable in one sentence

□ Tone is consistent with ACG expectations

□ Main characters have strong chemistry

□ Comedy and plot are balanced

□ References are accessible to the target niche

□ Author-reader interaction plan exists

If any are missing:

Revise.
