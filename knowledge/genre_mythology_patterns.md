# Mythological / Xuanhuan Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Male 16-40
* Cultivation, mythology, and world-building enthusiasts
* Readers who enjoy long-term progression and escalating power scales

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### The Power Scale

Cultivation novels are defined by their power systems. Every novel needs one:

| System Type | Mechanism | Strongest Feature |
|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| 丹田修炼 | Qi accumulation in dantian | Classic, easy to understand |
| 灵魂觉醒 | Soul-level power | Inner world depth |
| 法宝为主 | Artifact/cultivation tool focus | Item collection satisfaction |
| 血脉传承 | Bloodline abilities | Family legacy drama |
| 规则领悟 | Comprehension of world rules | Intellectual progression |
| 科技融合 | Technology + cultivation hybrid | Modern-world adaptation |

Every realm level must have:

- A name (练气/筑基/金丹/元婴...)
- Visible markers (aura, ability scope, social recognition)
- A clear threshold to the next level
- Consequences for failing to advance

### World Structure

| Layer | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| 世俗界 | Mortal world | Towns, empires, sects |
| 修真界 | Cultivation world | Sects, immortal mountains, demon territories |
| 仙界/神界 | Immortal realms | Heavenly courts, divine territories |
| 混沌/虚空 | Origin realms | Chaos sea, void, origin |

Each layer requires:

- New rules (different physics)
- New dangers (new enemy types)
- New opportunities (higher-grade resources)
- A reason to ascend (motivation for the protagonist)

## Sub-Variants

| Variant | Core Appeal | Key Conflict |
|---------|------------|-------------|
| 传统修仙 | Classic cultivation arc | Sect politics, tribulation, immortal ascension |
| 洪荒流 | Mythological world (女娲/盘古/鸿钧) | Mythological scale, fate vs free will |
| 凡人流 | Slow cultivation, resource scarcity | Survival, sect competition, mortality |
| 重生修仙 | Rebirth with future knowledge | Changing fate, correcting past mistakes |
| 系统修仙 | Cultivation + system panel | Visible progression, quest rewards |
| 异世穿越 | Transported to cultivation world | Culture clash, resource gap |
| 都市修仙 | Modern world + cultivation | Hidden immortal world, mortal complications |

## Selling Points

Strong opening angles:

* 他在高考考场醒来，发现自己回到了五百年前的修仙世界
* 系统报错，把他一个凡人丢进了洪荒时代——周围站着的，是还没成圣的诸位准圣
* 那一剑斩断了她的金丹，也斩断了她三百年的执念——重活一世，她只求长生
* 末法时代降临，所有修士的修为都在倒退，只有他发现——是自己体内那滴盘古精血在不断进化

## Genre Rules

### Sect Politics

Every major sect needs:

- A founding history (why the sect exists)
- Internal factions (competing interests)
- A peak of power (the current master/generation)
- A hidden weakness (the secret that could destroy it)
- A relationship with the protagonist (ally, rival, neutral)

### Tribulation / 天劫

Crossing a major realm requires tribulation. Design each tribulation as:

- A mini-arc with its own conflict
- A visible test of character (what the tribulation reveals)
- A consequence for failure (not just "retry")
- A reward that changes the story

### Enemy Design

Enemies should be:

- Motivated (not evil for evil)
- Powerful enough to threaten the protagonist at their current level
- Connected to a faction with resources
- Defeatable through cleverness, not just power level
- Remembered (give them a name, a face, a story)

### Romantic Subplot (if included)

- Must not dominate the cultivation arc
- She should be a cultivator with her own path
- Their relationship should strengthen both parties
- Competition and mutual growth over protection

---

## Pacing Notes

- Breakthrough every 20-50 chapters (early fast, late slow)
- Every major realm requires: tribulation + new ability + new enemies + new territory
- Resource scarcity drives early conflict; resource management drives late conflict
- Sect-level conflict every 100-200 chapters; world-level conflict every 500+ chapters
- The final breakthrough must feel earned through sacrifice, not convenient discovery

---

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 天劫降下，他却没有渡——他在等一个人，等了三百年，只为和她一起承受这最后一击
* 她挖开他的坟墓，里面空空如也——而天上的雷劫，正在向她的方向聚拢
* 师尊说他的资质永远无法突破筑基——但他发现，系统给的丹药可以无视这个限制
* 盘古残魂苏醒的那一刻，整个洪荒都在颤抖：「我等了无数个纪元，终于等到了一个能承受我力量的人」
