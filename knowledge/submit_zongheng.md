# submit_zongheng

## Purpose

Submission and packaging rules for Zongheng (纵横中文网).

Use when the target is Zongheng paid subscription / long serialization.

Pairs with knowledge/zongheng_patterns.md.

---

## When To Use

After submission_checklist and golden_three_reviewer pass.

Before Zongheng work creation and first batch upload.

---

## Platform Shape

Zongheng favors stable long serialization and paid readers who expect regular updates.

| Axis | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| Model | Paid subscription / membership |
| Reader expectation | Reliable cadence, long arcs |
| Title | Clear genre signal, identity advantage ok |
| Synopsis | Must state the long-term engine, not only the first win |

---

## Title Fit

Genre-legible titles outperform vague ones.

Identity advantage is welcome when the arc supports it.

---

## Synopsis Craft

- Line 1: who and what they lack
- Line 2: the advantage engine
- Line 3: the multi-hundred-chapter goal or conflict axis

Paid readers buy the engine, not only chapter 1.

---

## First Chapter Bar

Still needs an early hook, but world engine can be slightly denser than free platforms.

Do not skip the first concrete win.

---

## Review Risks

- Weak long-term engine (story dies after the first arc)
- Update promises the author cannot keep
- Category / tag mismatch

---

## Update Expectations

Silent gaps hurt more than on free platforms.

Publishing calendar and backlog safety line are mandatory before launch.

---

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Title signals genre and advantage
- [ ] Synopsis states the long engine
- [ ] Golden three GO/REVISE closed
- [ ] 5–10 chapter launch batch preferred
- [ ] Next month update plan fixed
