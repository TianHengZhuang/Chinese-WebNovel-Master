# Tadu Novel Success Patterns

Version: 1.0

Purpose:

Understand the hybrid free-plus-paid platform known for short-form quality.

Focus on:

- Concept originality
- Rapid twists
- Short-form excellence
- Suspense and brain-burning plots

---

# Core Principle

Tadu readers want to be surprised.

They tolerate shorter length if every chapter earns its place.

The platform rewards:

- Clever premises
- Unexpected reversals
- Tight prose
- Efficient storytelling

---

# Reader Profile

Readers appreciate craft and are drawn to distinctive voices.

Characteristics:

- Open to unconventional concepts
- Enjoy short-form and medium-length works
- Sensitive to filler
- Attracted to buzz-worthy premises

---

# Preferred Genres

Strengths:

- Suspense and thriller
- Mystery with twists
- Brain-burning (高智商) plots
- Science fiction concepts
- Short-form quality fiction
- Fantasy with a twist

---

# Opening Strategy

Open with:

- A question that cannot be ignored
- An anomaly the reader must understand
- A promise of secrets

First 1000 words are a pitch for the whole book.

---

# Chapter Craft

Chapter length: 1500-2500 words.

Every chapter should:

- Advance or complicate the mystery
- End on a turn
- Avoid padding

Twist discipline:

- Set up early
- Pay off later
- Never cheat the reader

---

# Suspense Design

Layer hooks:

- Immediate question
- Mid-range mystery
- Long-range secret

Plant details in chapter 1-3 that matter in chapter 20+.

---

# Tone Rules

Recommended tone:

- Sharp
- Controlled
- Playful when appropriate

Avoid:

- Purple prose
- Slow family melodrama
- Generic system novel repetition

---

# Title Patterns

Titles should intrigue without spoiling.

Formula:

Oddity + Promise

Examples:

- 第七个嫌疑人
- 我在末世开网吧
- 时间暂停后我无所不能
- 杀死那个穿越者

---

# Success Checklist

Before publishing:

□ Opening contains an irresistible question

□ Premise is pitchable in one sentence

□ Twist structure has early setup

□ Chapters are tight and filler-free

□ Tone is sharp and consistent

□ Long-range secret exists

If any are missing:

Revise.
