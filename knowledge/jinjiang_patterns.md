# Jinjiang Novel Patterns

## Audience

Primary Audience:

* Female 18-35
* Character-driven readers
* Emotion-first readers

Jinjiang readers read for the relationship and the voice.

Plot serves character, not the reverse.

---

## Core Characteristics

### Prose Quality

Readers expect:

* Control of language
* Distinct narrative voice
* Subtext over exposition

Rule:

A clumsy sentence costs more here than a slow plot.

---

### Character Depth

Readers want:

* Internal conflict
* Consistent motivation
* Growth that is earned

Rule:

Every character must want something and fear something.

---

### Emotional Tension

Structure:

* Setup of emotional stakes
* Misunderstanding or obstacle
* Turning point
* Resolution

Rule:

Emotion must be delayed, then paid in full.

---

### Relationship Architecture

The central relationship carries the story.

Requirements:

* Both sides must have agency
* Both sides must change
* Attraction must be justified

Rule:

A passive half of the pairing breaks the contract.

---

## Channel Conventions

Content is organized by channel:

| Channel | Focus | Reader Contract |
|---------|-------|-----------------|
| Romance | Male-female relationship | Emotional payoff |
| Pure Love | Same-sex relationship | Emotional payoff |
| No Couple | Plot or character study | No romance promised |

Rule:

Never cross a channel contract. Readers select by tag.

---

## Tag and Warning Culture

Readers rely on metadata:

* Ending type: HE / BE / OE
* Content warnings
* Tone markers: sweet / angst / healing

Rule:

Label honestly. Mislabeled tags are the fastest route to review backlash.

---

## Synopsis Craft

The synopsis is a sales page.

Structure:

Sentence 1: Hook or voice

Sentence 2: Conflict

Sentence 3: Promise

Rule:

The synopsis must sound like the book.

---

## Reader Retention Factors

Strong:

* Emotional tension
* Character voice
* Relationship progress
* Foreshadowed reveals

Weak:

* Flat characters
* Mechanical plot
* Info-dump openings
* Unearned reconciliation

---

## Success Checklist

Before publishing:

* Narrative voice established
* Both leads have goal, fear, weakness
* Channel contract respected
* Tags and warnings accurate
* Synopsis rewritten at least once
* First three chapters audited with tools/golden_three_reviewer.md

Rule:

Jinjiang is a craft culture. Voice is the only moat.
