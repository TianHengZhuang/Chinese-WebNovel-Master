# Zongheng Novel Success Patterns

Version: 1.0

Purpose:

Understand the paid-subscription model of a veteran male-oriented platform.

Focus on:

- Collection (收藏) conversion
- Subscription rate
- Long-term serialization
- Author welfare stability

---

# Core Principle

Zongheng is a traditional paid platform.

Readers pay per chapter, so trust is everything.

The contract is simple:

Stable updates in exchange for subscription loyalty.

---

# Reader Profile

Readers skew male and experienced.

Characteristics:

- Long web-fiction reading history
- Patient with setup when payoff is guaranteed
- Sensitive to update consistency
- Loyal to authors who deliver

They reward depth and world-building more than flashy openings.

---

# Preferred Genres

Traditional strengths:

- Xuanhuan (玄幻)
- Urban fantasy
- Historical
- Military
- Sci-fi
- Martial arts

Classical structures outperform experimental formats here.

---

# Opening Strategy

## Two-Layer Opening

Layer 1: Hook (chapter 1-3)

- Conflict or injustice that creates investment

Layer 2: Foundation (chapters 4-15)

- World rules
- Power system
- Long-term goals

Zongheng readers accept setup if the hook is solid.

## Recommended Opening Types

1. Low-status protagonist with a clear grievance
2. Secret identity revealed early
3. Life-and-death crisis that sets the stage

---

# Serialization Rules

Recommended cadence: 2-3 chapters per day, or 4000-6000 words daily.

Stability beats bursts:

- Consistent update time
- Reliable word count
- Planned arc boundaries

---

# Power System Design

Readers expect structured progression.

Include:

- Clear cultivation stages
- Visible power tiers
- Long-term ceiling
- Costs and limits

Power gains must be earned, not granted.

---

# Emotional Rhythm

Reward every 5-8 chapters.

Major arc payoff every 20-30 chapters.

Keep multiple threads:

- Main plot
- Relationship subplot
- Rivalry arc
- Mystery thread

---

# Title Patterns

Zongheng favors classical, confident titles.

Formula:

Subject + Scale + Promise

Examples:

- 万古神帝
- 剑来
- 完美世界
- 圣墟

---

# Commercial Optimization

Subscription success depends on:

- Strong first 10 chapters (trial period)
- Clear long-term direction
- Reliable pacing
- Consistent character voice

Avoid:

- Abrupt tone shifts
- Dropped plot threads
- Irregular updates

---

# Success Checklist

Before publishing:

□ Hook strong enough for 3-chapter trial

□ Power system clearly defined

□ Main arc can sustain 100+ chapters

□ Update schedule is realistic and stable

□ Character voice is consistent

□ Long-term mystery or goal exists

If any are missing:

Revise.
