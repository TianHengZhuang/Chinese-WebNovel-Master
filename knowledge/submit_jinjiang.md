# submit_jinjiang

## Purpose

Submission and packaging rules for Jinjiang Literature City (晋江文学城).

Use when the target is Jinjiang, especially romance, danmei, and female-frequency works.

---

## When To Use

After submission_checklist and title_scorer.

Before Jinjiang create-work and chapter upload.

---

## Platform Shape

Jinjiang privileges prose quality, relationship clarity, and tag honesty.

| Axis | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| Audience | Strong female-frequency and danmei core |
| Culture | Tag literacy is high; wrong tags get called out |
| Synopsis | Must signal pairing type and tone early |
| Warnings | Required for sensitive elements |
| Ranking | Mix of collection, comments, and update behavior |

---

## Title Fit

Emotional or relational titles work when they carry a concrete image.

Avoid male-frequency power-system title patterns unless the story truly is that.

---

## Synopsis Craft

State: protagonist identity, relationship premise, central conflict.

Signal: pairing orientation and tone (甜 / 虐 / 悬疑 / 成长).

Do not hide the pairing type that the tags already claim.

---

## Tags

- Pairing-related tags first if applicable
- Genre and setting second
- Trope tags last and only if accurate
- Never ship a tag the first 10 chapters contradict

---

## Content Warnings

List warnings that the platform and audience expect.

Incomplete warnings are a social and review risk.

---

## First Chapter Bar

Establish voice and relationship tension quickly.

Male-frequency "system popup in paragraph 2" openings often fail here unless the genre is explicit system fiction.

---

## Review Risks

- Tag / content mismatch
- Missing warnings
- Synopsis that overpromises a different pairing type
- Policy-sensitive content without required labels

---

## Update Expectations

Readers track authors closely. Silent gaps hurt.

Keep the publishing calendar visible in the author note when taking planned breaks.

---

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Title matches Jinjiang tone
- [ ] Synopsis signals pairing and conflict
- [ ] Tags honest and ordered
- [ ] Warnings complete
- [ ] Launch chapters match tag promises
