# Urban Supernatural / System Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Male 16-35 (core), male 18-40 (secondary)
* System-panel readers who enjoy visible progression mechanics
* Fans of power fantasy and wish-fulfillment

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### The System Panel

Readers expect a visible, structured layer:

- Attribute points (strength, speed, intelligence, spirit)
- Level or rank progression (青铜/白银/黄金/钻石...)
- Skill trees (unlockable abilities)
- Mission logs (main quest + branch quests)
- Inventory (items, materials, equipment)
- Title system (earned through achievement)

Panel updates are payoffs: show them only when something meaningful changes.

### System Types

| Type | Mechanism | Strongest Feature |
|------|-----------|-----------------|
| 天选系统 | System chooses the protagonist | Random rewards create surprise |
| 任务系统 | Complete quests for rewards | Clear goals, measurable progress |
| 升级系统 | Defeat enemies to gain EXP | Simple loop, addictive |
| 职业系统 | Choose a class and specialise | Build creativity |
| 合成系统 | Combine items for better ones | Collection satisfaction |
| 天赋系统 | Unique innate abilities | Character differentiation |
| 规则系统 | Master underlying world rules | Intellectual challenge |

## Selling Points

Strong opening angles:

* 破产那天，手机里突然出现一个APP——余额显示999999999元，但每消费一元才能解锁一元
* 末世降临，别人的系统是杀丧尸，只有他的系统让他治病救人
* 直播鉴宝十年无人问津，系统降临那一刻，他终于看清了那件青铜鼎上的铭文
* 女儿发烧住院，他却被绑定了奶爸系统——每哄好女儿一次，就涨一点体质

## Genre Rules

### System Discipline

Every system ability must have:

- A cost (cool-down, material, prerequisite quest, daily limit)
- A condition (completed mission, level threshold, item exchange)
- A visible result (panel update, skill unlock, title change)

A system that gives everything for free destroys tension.

### Progression Ladder

| Stage | Chapter Range | Content |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| 觉醒期 | Ch. 1-30 | System introduction, first abilities, early enemies |
| 成长期 | Ch. 31-100 | Multi-ability combos, rival systems, social ascent |
| 爆发期 | Ch. 101-300 | Power wars, faction conflicts, supernatural revelation |
| 终局期 | Ch. 300+ | System origin mystery, final antagonist, new world |

### Character Consistency

The system changes the protagonist's abilities, not their core personality.

A timid character does not become bold just because they gained power — the transformation must be gradual and caused by events.

### Rival Design

Every major rival needs:

- A system of their own (or a structural power source)
- A different skill build (reader can compare builds)
- A different motivation (not just "evil for evil")
- A reason they are dangerous (resources, connections, intelligence)

---

## Pacing Notes

- One visible gain per 1-3 chapters at minimum in early stage
- Mid-game: one major unlock every 10-20 chapters
- System upgrades must change the story, not just the panel numbers
- Every 50 chapters: a system-level revelation that recontextualises everything

---

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 系统提示音响起：「检测到宿主即将死亡，启动保命机制——消耗全部积蓄，换取一次重生机会」
* 他的系统面板突然变灰，弹出一行字：「本系统已转移至新宿主，祝您好运」
* 直播间的在线人数刚好卡在99999999——那是系统的上限，也是他命运的转折点
* 女儿画的画和古书上的记载一模一样——她不是普通的孩子
