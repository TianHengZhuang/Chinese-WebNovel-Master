# Sci-Fi Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Male 16-35
* Technology enthusiasts
* Hard-science and military-fiction fans
* Readers who value imagination + logic

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### World Building

Readers expect:

* A consistent future society
* Technology with visible rules and costs
* Science flavor that feels researched
* Scale: personal stakes inside a vast setting

### Power Fantasy Variants

- Black-tech system: protagonist obtains future knowledge
- Alien civilization contact: knowledge or resource advantage
- Interstellar empire building: territory, fleet, economy
- Post-apocalypse recovery: rebuild civilization with knowledge
- Near-future urban: secret tech in a modern world

---

## Selling Points

Strong opening angles:

* 未来军工科技：我提前点出了星海舰队 (future tech advantage)
* 从报废机甲维修师到银河军阀 (underdog with unique skill)
* 直播外星战场：地球人以为我在拍电影 (contrast + mystery)

## Genre Rules

### Technology Discipline

Every technology must have:

- Capability
- Limitation
- Cost

Readers reject magic labeled as science.

### Scale Ladder

Progress through scales:

Personal → Squad → Planetary → Interstellar

Each scale jump requires a new conflict, never just a number change.

### Alien Design

Aliens need:

- Logic (why they act)
- Culture (how they differ)
- Threat asymmetry (why they are dangerous)

Avoid: humanoids with forehead tattoos only.

---

## Pacing Notes

- Tech reveals are payoffs: do not dump blueprints
- Battles should change the situation, not just the body count
- Space battles need readable geometry (distance, formation, timing)

---

## Hook Formulas

* 开局获得 [unique advantage] + 立即 [visible gain] + 隐藏 [cost/threat]
* 已知地球命运 + 无法明说的身份 + 倒计时
