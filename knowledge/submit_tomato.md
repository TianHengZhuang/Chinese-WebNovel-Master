# submit_tomato

## Purpose

Submission and packaging rules for Tomato Novel (番茄小说) free / ad-supported distribution.

Use when the target is Tomato or a free-mode clone.

---

## When To Use

After submission_checklist metadata pack is ready.

Before Tomato author backend submission.

---

## Platform Shape

Tomato optimizes for completion rate and rapid hook density.

| Axis | Implication |
|------|-------------|
| Business model | Free reading; revenue from ads and distribution |
| Reader patience | Lower than paid male-frequency sites |
| Title style | Direct, high-concrete, often identity or conflict forward |
| Chapter length | Shorter effective beats win; avoid long setup |
| Cover | High contrast, readable at feed thumbnail size |

---

## Title Fit

Prefer concrete stakes and advantage.

Avoid literary or abstract titles.

Numbers and status words help (第一 / 无敌 / 万倍 / 退款).

---

## Synopsis Craft

One line advantage.

One line conflict.

One line immediate goal.

Cut every sentence that does not raise a question.

---

## First Chapter Bar

Hook in the opening paragraphs.

Payoff within the first chapter, not chapter five.

Minimize lore dumps; weave world facts through action.

---

## Tag and Category

Pick the category Tomato actually ranks the story in free feeds.

Do not copy Qidian tags blindly — free platforms weigh completion differently.

---

## Review and Policy Risks

- Banned themes and extreme violence thresholds differ from paid sites
- Clickbait title that does not match chapter 1 causes drop and may trigger review
- Cover text must match the in-app title rules

---

## Update Expectations

Batch-friendly. Readers expect frequent chapters.

Use publishing_calendar backlog safety line aggressively.

---

## Output Checklist

- [ ] Title concrete and free-feed native
- [ ] Synopsis <= 3 short sentences
- [ ] Chapter 1 delivers a visible win
- [ ] Cover brief readable at thumbnail
- [ ] Launch batch and next updates scheduled
