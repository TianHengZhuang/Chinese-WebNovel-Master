# Detective Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Male 20-45 (core), female 18-40 (secondary)
* Procedural, puzzle and hard-boiled readers
* Readers who stop to re-read the last three paragraphs

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### Case Architecture

The delivery unit is the case, not the chapter:

| Layer | Horizon | Function |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Case | 8-20 chapters | One crime, one solution |
| Volume arc | 60-120 chapters | Recurring antagonist or systemic rot |
| Series spine | Whole book | The protagonist's own unresolved case |

Close the case. Keep the spine open.

### The Three Lines

Run three lines at once in every volume:

- Case line: the crime and its chain of evidence
- Investigator line: what pursuing the case costs the protagonist
- Relationship line: partner, mentor, informant, family

If a line disappears for ten chapters, readers drop off.

### Fair Play Contract

* The reader must hold every clue needed to solve the case
* Withheld information is withheld in scene, never in narration
* The solution must be checkable against chapter text
* The lead stays one step ahead of the reader, not five

Break the contract once and re-reading stops. Re-reading is this genre's retention engine.

## Detective Toolbox

### Clue Types

| Type | Function | Failure Mode |
|------|----------|--------------|
| Physical | Anchors the scene | Too many; the reader cannot sort them |
| Testimonial | Adds motive and suspects | Liar with no tell |
| Forensic | Narrows the pool | A lab that answers everything |
| Behavioural | Reveals character | Mind-reading; unsupported intuition |

Plant at least one clue per scene that survives into the solution.

### Suspect Pool Math

* Open with four to six viable suspects
* Strike out one per three to four chapters, on evidence, not accident
* Keep at least two possible until the final quarter
* The answer must not eliminate a suspect the reader could not already eliminate

### Interrogation Rhythm

Scene structure: question → evasion → pressure → partial truth → cost.

* Every interrogation changes what the protagonist believes
* Confession is not the win; the verified finding is
* One interrogation per arc should fail outright

### Misdirection

* Red herrings are motivated: the suspect hides a real, smaller secret
* Every red herring resolves before the reveal
* Never misdirect by hiding a scene from the reader

## Genre Rules

### Evidence Chain Discipline

* Every deduction cites: source chapter → observation → inference
* Chain of custody is stated, or the solution fails
* Never let a new character hand over the final answer

### Procedure Authenticity

Readers forgive invention when it is internally consistent. They do not forgive procedure that contradicts itself two chapters later.

* Fix the jurisdiction and its limits before chapter 10
* Fix the protagonist's rank and authority; upgrade only at a visible cost
* Autopsy, lab and court timelines must survive arithmetic

### Power Advantage (金手指) Limits

| Advantage | Allowed Use | Required Cost |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| Super memory | Recall a planted detail | Cannot produce new information |
| Postcognition | Replay a scene | One piece missing each time |
| Psychometry | Read an object | Physical backlash |
| Perfect profiling | Narrow the suspects | Not proof; still needs admissible evidence |

A power shortens investigation. It never replaces deduction. If the power solves the case, the genre dies.

### Emotional Ratio

The case line carries retention; the personal line carries loyalty. Keep one scene of personal cost per three to four case scenes.

---

## Reader Retention Factors

* Case hook closed by chapter 2, larger question opened on the same page
* Suspect pool shrinks in visible steps
* Deadline pressure: the next victim, the press, the internal review
* Competence display every five chapters; one real mistake per arc
* An antagonist who deserves the protagonist

---

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 死者口袋里的房卡，登记的是主角自己的名字
* 嫌疑人说出了只有凶手才知道的细节——而她有不在场证明
* 结案报告交上去的第二天，卷宗里的照片全变了
* 他认出凶器是自己十年前丢失的那把刀
* 报案电话里，对方念的是他尚未公开的推演结论

---

## Pacing Notes

* Reveal one answer only when two new questions appear
* Escalate the cost of knowing: interference, threats, professional risk
* Alternate interrogation-heavy chapters with legwork chapters
* The last chapter of a case must change the protagonist's standing, not only close the file

---

## Success Checklist

* The case is solvable from chapter text alone
* All clues are planted before the reveal
* The suspect pool never re-opens without new evidence
* All three lines are present across the volume
* The power advantage never replaces deduction
* Procedure matches the stated jurisdiction and rank
* Every red herring is motivated and resolved
