# Era Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Female 25-45 (core), male 30+ (secondary)
* Nostalgia-driven readers
* Readers who enjoy life-detail texture and slow-burn satisfaction

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### Era Coordinates

Popular starting points:

- Late 1970s: reform opening, first opportunities
- 1980s: 万元户 era, scarcity + hope
- 1990s: layoffs, 下海, market chaos, internet dawn
- Early 2000s: real estate, WTO, mobile phones

Choose one coordinate and commit.

### Texture Over Plot

Era fiction wins on texture:

- Prices and wages (exact, verifiable)
- Objects (tickets, coupons, cassettes, 自行车)
- Media (songs, TV shows, newspapers)
- Policy milestones (household registration, 粮票, stock market events)

Wrong period detail breaks immersion instantly.

---

## Era Advantage Patterns (金手指)

| Type | Mechanism |
|------|-----------|
| Rebirth (重生) | Relive life with adult knowledge |
| Time travel (穿越) | Modern person enters the era |
| Future memory | Partial knowledge: prices, trends, events |

Rules:

- Advantage must feel earned, not omnipotent
- Every prediction needs a believable source (dream, memory fragment, expert cover)
- Rivals and authorities must react plausibly to "prophecy"

## Progression Types

- Family life upgrade: house → appliances → car
- Career path: worker → 个体户 → factory owner → industry leader
- Romance: slow courtship under era constraints
- Education: 高考 as life-changing event
- Real estate / stock: era-specific wealth windows

## Era Rules

### Accuracy

Verify before writing:

- Policy names and years
- Currency values
- Object availability by year
- Regional differences (city vs. countryside)

### Sensitivity

Avoid:

- Trivializing historical hardship
- Anachronistic values imposed by modern characters without conflict
- Political events as plot devices (keep family and life at center)

### Language

Dialogue should carry era flavor without becoming unreadable:

Period-appropriate terms, not full dialect transcription.

---

## Pacing Notes

- Material gains are the backbone payoffs: home, business, status
- Emotional payoffs (reconciliation, marriage, family reunion) every arc
- The reader should feel "I wish I could live that again" at least once per volume

## Hook Formulas

* 重回1985，她只想让母亲活过那个冬天
* 下岗第一天，她盘下了那间没人要的铺子
* 重生的他抢在所有人之前，买下了第一支股票
