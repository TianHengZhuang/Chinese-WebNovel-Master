# Food Genre Patterns

Purpose: write food fiction that makes a reader hungry, for both the healing-slice and the system-driven commercial strains.

Use together with the engine's own pack: genre_urban_supernatural_patterns.md (system/panel food), genre_era_crossover_patterns.md (era food / trade crossover).

---

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Female 18-45 (healing strain), male 18-40 (system/commercial strain)
* Readers who cook, eat out, or save food videos and recognize real technique
* Readers chasing the "one good bowl fixes a bad day" wish and the "small stall becomes an empire" wish

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### What The Food Reader Wants

* A dish the reader can smell, see, hear, and almost taste — never just "好吃"
* A protagonist who earns the craft: technique, ingredients, timing, not magic
* The eater's reaction as the payoff: one mouthful that changes a mood, a memory, a decision
* A sense of place and culture: a cuisine, a region, a family recipe worth protecting

### What Breaks The Illusion

* A dish praised with no sensory detail
* A golden finger that removes the cooking instead of sharpening it
* Every customer reacting with the same stock exclamation
* Cuisine mistakes a real cook would catch (wrong technique, wrong ingredient, wrong season)

## The Sensory Engine

### One Dish, Five Senses

Describe every signature dish through at least three senses:

* Sight: colour, gloss, the steam, the plating
* Sound: the sizzle, the crack, the broth's first boil
* Smell: the head note, the base note, the after-note
* Taste: the first hit, the mid, the finish
* Touch / mouthfeel: crisp, tender, springy, melting

### The Reaction As Payoff

The eater's reaction is the genre's applause:

* Give the reaction a context: a memory, a deadline, a person they miss
* Make the change visible: a decision made, a grudge dropped, a family reconciled
* Vary the reaction; never let two customers repeat the same line

---

## Craft And Ingredients

* Name the technique (刀工, 火候, 收汁, 发酵) and the ingredient's origin and season
* One hard-won craft point per dish: a timing, a ratio, a technique the protagonist had to learn
* Ingredients have limits: season, cost, supply, freshness — they create plot, not decoration

---

## Golden Finger (金手指) Limits

| Advantage | Allowed Use | Required Cost |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| System / panel | Recipe unlocks, skill visibility | Still needs real ingredients and technique |
| Inherited craft | A family recipe or secret | Protect it; a rival wants it too |
| Perfect palate | Taste and reproduce a dish | Cannot invent a missing ingredient |
| Space / inventory | Stores scarce ingredients | Volume limited; secrecy risk |

The golden finger supplies a recipe or a sense. It never removes the fire, the ingredient, or the customer who can walk away.

## Genre Rules

### Show The Craft, Not The Label

* Replace "这道菜好吃得让人流泪" with the sequence: the sear, the aromatics, the first bite, the memory it triggers
* The losing dish must be as legible as the winning one — what went wrong and why

### Competence Cadence

* Signature dish shown inside the first three chapters, with a customer changed by it
* One new dish or technique per 3-5 chapters
* One real failure per arc: a burnt batch, a wrong ingredient, a bad review

### Failure Modes

| Failure | Symptom | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Empty praise | "好吃" with no detail | Rewrite through three senses |
| Magic stove | Golden finger replaces cooking | Keep technique and ingredients as the cost |
| Clone reactions | Customers repeat the same line | Give each reaction a distinct context |
| Cuisine error | Wrong technique or ingredient | Audit against real cooking |
| Scale without pain | Chain grows with no quality cost | Add a scale tax: supply, training, dilution |

---

## Reader Retention Factors

* Signature dish shown with sensory detail inside the first three chapters
* One customer visibly changed per arc, in a distinct, non-repeated way
* One new dish or technique every 3-5 chapters
* A craft or ingredient constraint that creates plot, not decoration
* One real failure and recovery per arc

---

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 她把那碗汤端到客人面前时，对方刚准备掀桌子——第一口之后，满屋子都安静了
* 菜单上消失三十年的那道菜，今晚被一个陌生客人点了出来
* 他闻了闻那锅汤，报出了连老板都忘了的第九味香料
* 米其林评委坐进小店的那天，后厨的煤气刚好断了
* 对面连锁店开业的鞭炮还没响，他家门口已经排到了街尾

---

## Pacing Notes

* Alternate a dish chapter with a customer-story chapter; never two pure-cooking chapters in a row
* Build a dish: need → ingredient → setback → technique → reveal → reaction
* Save the empire-level payoff for an arc finale; let each dish or customer change the shop's standing
* One emotional payoff per volume: a memory surfaced, a family reconciled, a rival tasted defeat

---

## Success Checklist

* Every signature dish is described through at least three senses
* The golden finger supplies a recipe or sense, never removes the cooking
* A signature dish with a changed customer inside the first three chapters
* Customer reactions are distinct and contextual, never cloned
* Cuisine, technique and ingredients survive a cook's skim
* One new dish or technique every 3-5 chapters
* One real failure and recovery per arc
