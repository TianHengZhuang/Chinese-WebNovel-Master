# Historical / Time-Travel Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Female 20-45 (core), male 18-40 (secondary)
* Readers who enjoy period atmosphere and slow-burn storytelling
* Fans of emotional warmth, family dynamics, and life-under-difficulty narratives

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### Historical Coordinates

Popular starting eras:

| Era | Starting Point | Key Opportunities |
|-----|--------------|-------------------|
| Late Qing | 1880s-1911 | Reform, revolution, foreign invasion |
| Republic | 1912-1949 | War, entrepreneurship, espionage |
| Early PRC | 1949-1978 | Land reform, industrialisation, collectivisation |
| Reform era | 1978-2000 | Market economy, stock market, urbanisation |
| Contemporary | 2000-present | Real estate, internet, global integration |

Choose one era and commit fully. Cross-era details must be verifiable.

### Period Texture

Readers notice:

- Exact prices and wages (year-appropriate, region-specific)
- Daily objects (coupons, ration books, typewriters, fax machines)
- Media (radio, TV shows, songs, newspapers, web portals)
- Policy events (household registration, tax reforms, market openings)
- Social customs (greetings, dress codes, family structures)

Wrong detail = immersion break. Right detail = reader trusts the world.

## Selling Points

Strong opening angles:

* 她重生在1978年高考前夜，这一次她要改写家族的命运
* 他穿越成赘婿，面对抄家之危，用未来知识在晚清步步为营
* 携商场记忆回1985，他用一台收音机换来了第一桶金
* 重生回知青下乡那年，她在东北雪原上建起了第一个工厂

## Genre Rules

### Period Research

Before writing:

- Verify policy names and dates
- Check object availability by year and region
- Confirm currency values (yuan, jiao, fen; inflation context)
- Review social customs (not impose modern values without conflict)

### Knowledge Advantage (金手指)

When protagonist has future knowledge:

- Every prediction needs a plausible cover (dream, teacher, old book)
- Timing is everything: too early = suspicious; too late = useless
- Rivals and authorities must plausibly react to "prophecy"
- Avoid omnipotent: mistakes and partial knowledge create tension

### Romance in Historical

Slow-burn relationship arc is expected:

- First encounter → shared hardship → growing trust → confession → obstacles → reunion
- Family opposition is a genre requirement, not an obstacle
- Emotional payoff must feel earned, not rushed

### Status Progression

Social mobility patterns:

- Peasant → village cadre → township → county → city → province → national
- Or: Student → worker → technician → manager → entrepreneur
- Each level requires a visible change in daily life, not just a number

---

## Pacing Notes

- Material upgrades (home, food, clothing, appliances) are backbone payoffs
- Emotional payoffs (family reunion, marriage, reconciliation) every arc
- Policy changes as plot pivots (reform opens opportunities, downturns create conflict)
- The reader should feel "I wish I could live that again" at least once per volume

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 他刚想说出口，却被告知——他父亲就是当年害死她全家的那个人
* 她的胭脂盒里藏着一张1956年的粮票，而她记得自己是2008年穿越来的
* 公社书记来查账，她把账本藏在墙缝里——那是她用二十年后的会计知识做的两套账
* 离婚协议书上签完字，她走进1982年的第一个早晨——她知道房价会在三十年后翻一百倍
