# Feilu Novel Patterns

## Audience

Primary Audience:

* Male 16-30
* Mobile-first readers
* Fragmented reading sessions

Feilu readers read in short bursts.

They reward speed and punish delay.

---

## Core Characteristics

### Extreme Pacing

Every chapter must deliver at least one payoff.

Setup chapters do not survive here.

Rule:

If a chapter has no payoff, it is dead weight.

---

### High Payoff Density

Readers expect:

* Frequent wins
* Fast escalation
* Immediate satisfaction

Rule:

Deliver a win every chapter, a big win every three.

---

### Transparent Power Fantasy

Readers want:

* Direct wish fulfillment
* Obvious advantage
* Visible domination

Rule:

Never hide the advantage from the reader.

---

### Fan-Fiction Culture

A large share of the catalog is derivative work.

Common sources:

* Anime
* Film and television
* Games
* Comics

Rule:

Derivative works must satisfy the source audience first.

---

### Volume Discipline

Serialization is measured in output.

Readers expect daily updates in volume.

Rule:

Consistency of output beats perfection of any single chapter.

---

## Chapter Economics

Readers pay per chapter.

Every chapter is a purchase decision.

Therefore:

* Start with a payoff, not a recap
* End with an open loop
* Never pad for length

---

## Reader Retention Factors

Strong:

* Immediate payoff
* Rapid escalation
* Clear advantage
* Novelty of premise

Weak:

* Long setup
* Slow-burn romance
* Extensive world building
* Philosophical reflection

---

## Success Checklist

Before publishing:

* Premise stated in the title
* First chapter delivers a payoff
* Payoff density verified across the first ten chapters
* Escalation ladder defined
* Chapter endings hooked
* Title scored against the platform convention

Rule:

Feilu is a sprint culture. Momentum is the only asset.
