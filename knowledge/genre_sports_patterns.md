# Sports Genre Patterns

Purpose: write competitive-sport fiction that reads like a live broadcast and keeps a reader who does not know the sport.

Use together with the engine's own pack: genre_game_patterns.md (system/panel sports), genre_urban_supernatural_patterns.md (supernatural sports).

---

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Male 16-40 (core), female 16-30 (secondary)
* Fans of the sport who fact-check the box score, and casual readers who only want the comeback
* Readers chasing the "underdog rises" wish and the last-second winner

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### What The Sports Reader Wants

* A protagonist who is provably better at one thing, and is tested on it every match
* Real scorelines, real rules, real stakes — the match must survive a fan's fact-check
* One signature skill that the protagonist owns, plus visible weaknesses that take time to close
* Teammates who matter, opponents who deserve to win, and a rivalry that spans seasons

### What Breaks The Illusion

* A scoreline or stat line no real player could produce
* A golden finger that removes competition instead of sharpening it
* Matches told as summaries instead of moments
* Every opponent folding the first time the protagonist shows up

## Sport-Specific Engine

### Match Realism

Every match must satisfy a fan, not just a plot outline:

* Fix the sport's real rules, positions, and scoring before chapter 1
* Use the sport's real vocabulary in narration (战术, 越位, 挡拆, 换人名额)
* Scorelines, minutes, and individual stats must survive arithmetic
* The protagonist's best play must be legal under the stated rules

### Signature Skill + Weakness

* Give the protagonist one elite trait the reader can picture: a shot, a burst, a read
* List the two or three weaknesses that lose matches until fixed
* Upgrade the skill only after a visible cost: injury, technique rebuild, a coach's demand

### Team / Rivalry Economy

* At least two teammates with their own agendas
* One rival who improves in parallel and meets the protagonist at fixed milestones
* A coach or veteran whose approval is not automatic

---

## Golden Finger (金手指) Limits

| Advantage | Allowed Use | Required Cost |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| System / training panel | Structured training, stat visibility | Cannot replace live-match instinct |
| Rebirth | Knows a result, a pick, a trend | Cannot re-watch the match in advance |
| Talent / physique | Higher ceiling | Still needs years of technique |
| Data / analytics eye | Reads a formation, a weakness | Still needs teammates to execute |

The golden finger raises the ceiling. It never removes the opponent, the referee, or the clock.

## Genre Rules

### Competence Display Rhythm

* Show the signature skill inside the first three chapters, in a real or trial match
* Every 5-8 chapters, one full match with a clear win, loss, or learning
* The protagonist must lose or struggle at least once per arc, against a named rival

### Stakes Escalation

Escalate in fixed layers: personal (a promise) → team (a roster spot) → city / nation (a title) → legacy (an era).

Never jump a layer without paying the one below it.

### Failure Modes

| Failure | Symptom | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Invincible protagonist | Every match is a blowout | Add a rival, an injury, a rule change |
| Stat abuse | Unrealistic numbers | Audit the stat line against real records |
| Match summary | Results without moments | Rewrite as one scene with a turning point |
| Opponent flattening | Rivals fold on sight | Give the rival a real win and a real grudge |
| Romance creep | Romance buries the match | Keep one match scene per chapter minimum |

---

## Reader Retention Factors

* Signature skill shown inside the first three chapters
* One full, suspenseful match every 5-8 chapters
* A rival who improves on-screen and meets the protagonist at milestones
* Personal stakes stated before every big match
* A named cost (injury, failure, betrayal) the protagonist pays per arc

---

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 裁判哨响的前一秒，他把球从自己的半场扔了出去——进了
* 全场都在等他退役，而他的体检报告上写着"巅峰期还没开始"
* 对手把夺冠庆功宴订在今晚，却不知道替补席上坐着那个被赶走的人
* 教练给的最后十秒，他打出了训练里从未成功过的那个战术
* 队医悄悄告诉他：再打一场，你的跟腱就断了

---

## Pacing Notes

* Alternate training chapters with match chapters; never two pure-training chapters in a row
* Build to a match: stakes → preparation → setback → the match → fallout
* Save the dynasty-level payoff for a season finale; make every match end by changing standing
* One non-sport emotional payoff per volume: family, friendship, or a rivalry resolved

---

## Success Checklist

* The match survives a fan's fact-check on rules and numbers
* The protagonist owns one elite trait and two or three named weaknesses
* A rival improves in parallel and meets the protagonist at milestones
* One full match with a turning point every 5-8 chapters
* The golden finger raises the ceiling, never removes the opponent
* Stakes escalate one layer at a time
* At least one real loss or struggle per arc
