# Romance / Pure Love Genre Patterns

## Audience

Primary Readers:

* Female 15-40 (core)
* Readers who value emotional journey, character growth, and relationship depth
* Fans of slow-burn, enemies-to-lovers, and mutual growth arcs

---

## Core Reader Expectations

### The Relationship Arc

Romance lives or dies on the relationship arc:

| Stage | Reader Expectation | Chapter Position |
|-------|-------------------|-----------------|
| First encounter | Memorable, slightly antagonistic | Ch. 1-3 |
| Growing closer | Daily life intimacy, small gestures | Ch. 4-15 |
| First confession | Tension breaks, emotional peak | Ch. 15-30 |
| Separation/Obstacle | External or internal force | Ch. 30-60 |
| Reunion | Earned payoff, new depth | Ch. 60-80 |
| Long-term commitment | Shared future, final hook | Ch. 80+ |

Each stage requires visible character growth, not just feelings.

### Emotional Resonance

Readers want to feel:

- The moment attraction shifts from intellectual to emotional
- The specific small gesture that breaks the ice
- The internal conflict between head and heart
- The relief when the misunderstanding clears

If readers can skip the relationship scenes without losing anything — the scenes are not working.

## Genre Rules

### The Love Triangle

If used:

- Rival must be genuinely compelling (not a cardboard antagonist)
- Protagonist's choice must be driven by character, not plot convenience
- The "losing" party must have a satisfying arc of their own
- Never draw out the choice beyond chapter 100

### Misunderstanding Management

Romance thrives on misunderstandings — but they must be:

- Believable (a real person could make this mistake)
- Not repeated (the same misunderstanding twice is lazy writing)
- Resolved with character growth (understanding changes both people)
- Never resolved by a third party explaining everything

### Physical Intimacy

- Build tension before the first kiss (minimum 15 chapters of anticipation)
- Emotional intimacy > physical intimacy as the primary payoff
- Intimacy scenes should reveal character, not just describe action
- Fade-to-black is acceptable; explicit content platform rules apply

### Female Protagonist (女频)

Empowerment patterns:

- Career competence + romantic vulnerability
- She has goals beyond the relationship
- She makes decisions, not just reacts
- The relationship makes her stronger, not dependent

### Male Protagonist (男频言情)

Protection fantasy patterns:

- He protects, but also respects her agency
- He grows because of her, not just because of training
- His wealth/status is the backdrop, not the point
- The relationship is the arena where both characters shine

---

## Pacing Notes

- One emotional peak every 10-15 chapters
- Relationship progress should be measurable: first name → last name → intimate name
- Physical setting matters: each location carries emotional weight
- Songs, weather, and objects are emotional anchors (use sparingly but deliberately)
- The final chapter should resolve the relationship AND open a new chapter of shared life

## Chapter Hook Patterns

* 她签完离婚协议转身就走，没有回头——但他看见了她包里露出的那张检查报告
* 第一次见面，他就认出了她——十年前她在雨里等他的那个女孩，和现在站在被告席上的她
* 订婚宴上，她的手机亮了：「您的股票账户已亏损97%。」发件人：她的未婚夫
* 他在ICU门外等了三天三夜，等来的是一张器官捐献志愿书——她签的名字旁边，多了一个他的名字
